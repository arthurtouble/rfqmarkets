# Candidate accounting and incident semantics

This is an implementation specification, not independent approval of its economics. It describes the candidate being tested and leaves cap calibration/release qualification in the production plan.

All balances/PnL are signed USDC micro-units. A customer gain debits on-chain maker backing; a customer loss credits it. Insurance is used only after realizing an account-wide default, not to erase temporary negative cash collateral while an offsetting position remains open. External hedge capital is never assumed immediately available to settle Base obligations.

## Bankruptcy and offsets

Liquidation requires fresh authenticated prices for all open portfolio legs. Settle both markets' outstanding funding as one net transfer before assessing cross-margin maintenance equity. If equity is nonpositive, close every position at the stored executable side (long bid / short ask), net all PnL before debiting maker backing, and then apply the default waterfall once. A keeper's selected market cannot choose which offset survives or change the bankrupt account's net realized loss.

For positive-equity partial liquidation, use the existing bounded liquidation-size formula. If that close produces negative cash collateral, realize the remaining portfolio before applying any default absorption. Collect the liquidation penalty only from remaining positive collateral, based on total closed executable notional. Insurance receives penalty less keeper reward. Negative collateral after every leg is closed is reset to zero, first debiting insurance, then maker backing; uncovered loss triggers global resolution. The waterfall rejects any attempt to reset a negative balance with a position still open.

All-leg bankruptcy is netted before checking maker affordability. Individual partial/owner closes retain their individual settlement semantics; an unfunded individual winner can trigger resolution even if other, unclosed customers might later realize offsetting losses. This is a conservative liquidity incident policy, not proof of economic insolvency across the entire hedge portfolio.

A paused owner close preserves negative cash collateral while another position remains open. After the last position closes, apply the same default waterfall exactly once and emit its insurance/maker/uncovered amounts. A remaining winner can restore positive collateral without consuming insurance. A fully closed losing account must not retain debt that permissionless position liquidation can no longer reach. Owner closes do not collect a liquidation penalty or keeper reward.

## Maker settlement exhaustion

Before transferring any realized PnL or funding credit to a customer, compare the credit with available maker backing. If insufficient, commit resolution and preserve the unpaid position/funding for resolution valuation. Do not mint unfunded collateral, take a trade fee, consume a trade nonce or emit a successful trade/close event for the unpaid operation. Funding already settled earlier in the transaction remains settled and is represented by its updated position funding index.

Sponsored owner actions consume their action nonce before attempting their operation. An action transaction can therefore consume its nonce and trigger resolution without paying a withdrawal or closing a position. API responses must inspect the matching settlement event, not infer payment/fill merely from status=1. The direct exit application also must distinguish resolution-triggering receipts. Resolution claims use their separate owner action and cannot be authorized with a session key.

## Funding clock and resolution cutoff

The existing index formula uses the validated mark supplied to a funding update, capped signed APR and the complete interval since its stored funding time. It applies that mark/rate to the interval; it does not reconstruct historical prices or skew. Large offline intervals require explicit economic review of this approximation. The candidate removes the old seven-day truncation, uses full-precision multiply/divide with signed truncation toward zero, and advances funding time to the exact update time in one bounded computation.

At the first resolution trigger, advance both indices to the trigger's block timestamp using their previously authenticated cached marks and existing aggregate skew/policy. A never-priced, unused market has zero mark and accrues zero. This is a defined cached-mark incident valuation, not an unsigned fallback oracle or a promise that historical funding is accurate. Neither later oracle refresh nor liquidation may change funding/positions after resolution begins. Oracle replacement is blocked during resolution price sampling.

For each customer, resolution equity is stored collateral plus side-independent median resolution price PnL minus outstanding funding against the frozen trigger indices. Claims clamp negative equity to zero. Processing order is the registered-account list; keeper batch size and time after the trigger cannot alter claims. Three authenticated post-trigger observations spanning at least 30 seconds determine each market's median. Final assets are the actual Base USDC balance, distributed pro rata when below claims; separately supplied recoveries increase entitlements under the existing bounded recovery mechanism.

## Library/storage boundary

RFQRiskMath contains portfolio/account arithmetic plus bounded clearing bookkeeping. The exposure extension, signer rotation, portfolio clearing, funding-index recording and resolution-account crystallization receive explicit typed storage references; exact token pulls execute in the proxy delegatecall context. Core functions retain authorization, reentrancy guards, PnL affordability checks, scalar capital/collateral ledgers and incident transitions. This library now writes storage and must be reviewed as part of the implementation authority boundary. No helper uses hard-coded storage slots or slot assembly. RFQSignatureVerifier is a new read-only linked authorization module. Its domain separator executes in the clearing proxy delegatecall context and retains the name/version/chain/proxy and unchanged EIP-712 schemas. Nonce/session/signer storage references retain original slots. Original storage declarations and their ordering remain unchanged; the ExposureControls struct is appended after marketLimitWord. See [exposure controls and migration](EXPOSURE-CONTROLS-AND-MIGRATION.md) for upgrade admission and exit invariants.

Both linked addresses and their exact deployed code must be recorded and independently reviewed for every candidate. Deploying an implementation must fail when either link is absent. The additional library deployment changes the predicted proxy nonce offset from three to four; adapter authorization must still bind the actual predicted proxy. Existing manifests must not be silently treated as candidates for the new implementation. An old proxy has uninitialized exposure counters and cannot trade or unpause until the bounded migration completes. Governance pause and policy configuration fence older maker approvals; still-valid owner intents retain their domain/signature format and can be reapproved under the new epoch/policy.

Gross/side/capital admission and bounded migration are implemented in the subsequent exposure candidate. The subsequent [gross reservation candidate](../docs/architecture/gross-approval-reservations.md) accounts for escaped directional approvals and their net/stress execution envelope. `declareResolution` remains governance-authorized for a paused market and also permits any caller to prove objective undercapitalization while open exposure exists; empty unfunded deployments are not triggerable. Exact pending realized-capital debits, economic calibration, Byzantine reservation coordination and production hedge qualification remain unfinished.
